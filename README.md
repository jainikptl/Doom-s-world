# 🏰 Doom's World

**The Digital Kingdom of Doctor Doom** — A modern, full-stack recruitment platform themed around Doctor Doom's BattleWorld, featuring real-time chat, swipe-based candidate selection, video interviews, and comprehensive candidate management.

🌐 **Live Site:** [doom-s-world.vercel.app](https://doom-s-world.vercel.app)

🔍 **Project Overview** - Doom's BattleWorld Recruitment is an innovative recruitment platform that gamifies the hiring process. Built with a Marvel-inspired theme, this platform connects candidates (heroes and villains) with Doom (the recruiter) through an interactive, swipe-based interface reminiscent of modern dating apps but designed for professional recruitment.

---
## 📖 Usage Guide

### 👤 For Candidates (Interviewees)

1. **Sign Up** as a candidate  
2. **Complete your profile** with all required details (info, powers, abilities, etc.)  
3. **Browse jobs** on the job board  
4. **Apply** to interesting positions  
5. **Get shortlisted** (chat unlocks after recruiter approval)  
6. **Engage with recruiters** through real-time chat  
7. **Attend interviews** via the integrated video system  
8. **Receive Demo Tasks** after interviews (not real assignments, just demo challenges)  
9. **Earn Points or Penalties** based on task performance  
10. **Track Progress** on the Candidate Leaderboard to see rankings  
11. **Improve Skills & Reputation** with every task and interview  

---

### 👑 For Recruiters (Doom)

1. **Sign Up** as a recruiter  
2. **Post jobs** with detailed descriptions and requirements  
3. **Review applications** using the swipe interface  
4. **Shortlist candidates** by swiping right (reject with left)  
5. **Chat** with shortlisted candidates instantly  
6. **Schedule interviews** and send email notifications  
7. **Conduct video interviews** directly on the platform  
8. **Assign Demo Tasks** to shortlisted candidates  
9. **Evaluate Performance** with points, penalties, and leaderboard impact  
10. **Monitor Analytics** for candidate performance, engagement, and hiring pipeline  


## 🚀 Features

- **👥 Dual Role System**  
  Separate portals for Candidates and Recruiters with tailored features.

- **🔐 Authentication & Security**  
  Role-based login, protected routes, session management, and data validation.

- **📝 Candidate Profile Management**  
  Detailed profiles with personal info, abilities, weaknesses, achievements, and specialization. Includes profile picture upload and form validation.

- **💼 Job Posting System**  
  Recruiters can post jobs with categories, requirements, and track applications in real time.

- **📱 Smart Application System**  
  One-click applications, resume uploads, and status tracking.

- **👆 Swipe-Based Candidate Review**  
  Swipe left to reject, right to shortlist, save for later, or favorite candidates with smooth card animations.

- **💬 Real-Time Chat System**  
  Messaging between recruiters and shortlisted candidates with notifications, chat history, and status indicators.

- **🎥 Video Interview Integration**  
  In-app video calling with scheduling, and no external apps required.

- **📧 Email Notification System**  
  Automated emails for interviews, shortlisting, and status updates with customizable templates.

- **📊 Candidate Leaderboard**  
  Gamified ranking system with points, competitor analysis, and performance tracking.

- **📅 Interview Calendar Management**  
  Availability settings, scheduling, time zone support, and conflict prevention.

- **🎨 Modern UI/UX Design**  
  Responsive design, dark theme, animations, swipe gestures, and professional aesthetics.

- **🔧 Scalable Architecture**  
  Modular structure, REST APIs, optimized database, performance monitoring, and error logging.

- **📈 Advanced Analytics**  
  Dashboards for recruitment metrics, engagement tracking, conversion rates, and insights.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React.js, Tailwind CSS  
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js  
- **Database:** MongoDB  
- **Hosting:** Vercel  
- **AI Integration:** OpenAI API, NLP models
